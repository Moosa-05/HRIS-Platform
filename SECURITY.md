# Security Policy

## Responsible Disclosure

We prioritize the security and data privacy of HRIS Platform. If you discover a potential vulnerability or security concern, we appreciate your cooperation in reporting it responsibly.

**Please do NOT disclose vulnerabilities publicly via GitHub issues or discussions.**

---

## Reporting a Security Concern

To report a vulnerability:

- **Email:** [realmoosaa@gmail.com](mailto:realmoosaa@gmail.com)
- **Subject:** `[Security Report] HRIS Platform`

Please include:
- A clear summary of the issue.
- Step-by-step reproduction instructions or proof of concept.
- Relevant details regarding the environment or scenario.

Reports are reviewed promptly and addressed in authorized updates.
